Game overview

What you need to know before you roll.

Who it’s for

University students

Ages 18 and up.

Players

Up to 6

Everyone moves through all four rooms.

Time

About 60 min

One full game across the whole campus map.

The point

Have fun

And get to know your friends better.

Who starts?

At the start of each mini game, everyone rolls two dice. Highest roll starts and play goes clockwise. Tie for highest? Those players roll again.

The four rooms

Each room is its own mini game, set in a real corner of campus.

Ick Room

Drama & theatrics.

Gossip Room

Communication & giggles.

The Gym

To burn off one too many campus donuts.

Music Room

Do you really know the words to that song?

The board is inspired by real spots at the University of Pretoria: the Drama building, the Piazza, and the sports centre.

Ick Room

Draw an Ick card and act it out without words before the 30-second timer runs out. A correct guess moves both the actor and guesser three spaces forward. If nobody guesses correctly, the actor moves one space back. The room ends when the first player reaches the finish; everyone records a grade based on their position.

Gossip Room

The judge reads a fill-in-the-blank gossip prompt. Everyone else submits an amplification card face down. The judge shuffles and reads the combinations, then chooses a favourite. That player earns the card’s scandal points. Refill your hand and rotate the judge until everyone has had a turn. The highest total score wins.

The Gym

Roll the dice and follow the fitness journey, gaining or losing strength in arms, legs, abs and full body. Use those scores to build your Gym Bro and its balancing platform. Then roll for weight size: even means a big shape, odd means a little one. Draw a weight without looking and stack it on your platform. Drop a weight or topple your Gym Bro and you are out. Last bro standing wins.

Music Room

Flip the 30-second timer and sing lyrics containing the words on your card, or finish the lyrics it starts. Move forward according to the lyrics you complete. If you get stuck, draw one extra card: a maximum of two cards per turn. Discard used cards and reshuffle when the deck runs out. Keep playing until the first three players reach the finish.

Report cards

Every room ends with a grade.

Like the academic records we get in varsity, each player fills in their student info and records a grade for their finishing position in every room. Add up the points, work out your percentage, and the highest average wins the game.

First place earns an A (25 points), second a B (18), third a C (10), and fourth or lower an F (5). Four rooms make a total out of 100.

Highest average wins.
